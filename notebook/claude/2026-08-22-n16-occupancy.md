# 2026-08-22 — n16 occupancy exhaustion (worker O2, issue #97)

Lane: generalise the 15-piece pigeonhole to *covering with slack* — cover $T_a$ by
$N \ge 16$ pieces of diameter $<1$, enumerate the $\binom{N}{16}$ occupancy patterns, refute
each by exact pair pruning, capacity pruning (cited $a_k$, $k \le 15$ only), and recursive
refinement. Target: any exactly certified $a > 1+2\sqrt3$.

Everything below is `sketch`/`numerical` (my own work, unreviewed). Code:
`experiments/packing-n16-occupancy/`. Write-up: `attacks/n16-occupancy/README.md`.

## Notes to self / for the reviewer

- The clique observation that shaped the design: with all-diameter-$<1$ pieces, any pattern
  refuted by *pairs alone* corresponds to merging two pieces of joint diameter $<1$, i.e. to
  a 15-piece covering — so pure pair pruning can never beat $1+2\sqrt3$. All new value must
  come from capacity subsets ($k \ge 4$ pieces in a triangle of side $< a_k$) or refinement.
- Boundary counting says the corner-capacity variant (cap-3 corner triangles of side
  $< \sqrt3 = a_4$) hits the *same* $1+2\sqrt3$ wall: a corner triangle covers $\le \sqrt3$ of
  each side, exactly what the corner-quad + edge-pentagon chain of the 15-cover covers with
  the same 3 points; and for $a > 1+2\sqrt3$ each side's middle gap $a - 2\sqrt3 > 1$ forces
  two extra pieces per side. Both routes are the unit triangular lattice in disguise. Sketch
  only; recorded in the attack README.
- Cost heuristic re-derived from `circle-packing-bnb`: closing at $a$ requires refuting
  phantom configurations at pairwise $\ge 1-\delta$, $\delta \approx 1 - a/a_{16}$, needing
  cells of diameter $O(\delta)$. With $a_{16}$ anywhere in $[4.4641, 4.6248]$, $\delta \le
  0.033$ at $a = 4.47$ — 5+ refinement levels on each of 16 cells. Expectation set
  accordingly: the probe is a probe, not a promise.
