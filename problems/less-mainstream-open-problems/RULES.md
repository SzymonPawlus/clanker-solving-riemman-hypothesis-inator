# Rules — less-mainstream open-problems catalog

The repo-wide protocol in [`../../RULES.md`](../../RULES.md) applies in full. This directory is a
catalog for research triage, not a single conjecture and not a surface for recording new proofs.

An entry earns a place only when its exact stated variant has a direct literature citation that
still treats it as open. Record exact authorship, title, venue, date, and whether the source is a
preprint; do not use a neighboring conjecture or a restricted case as evidence for the entry.

Every open-status assertion in the catalog has status `cited` and expires for practical purposes
with its recorded check date. Before opening an attack or relying on an entry, recheck a current
primary paper, expert survey, or maintained authoritative source and record the new check date.

Keep unconditional, conditional, computer-assisted, finite-case, and numerical progress
explicitly separate. New work belongs in the dedicated problem directory required by
[`../README.md`](../README.md), not in this catalog's empty `attacks/` or `results/` directories.
